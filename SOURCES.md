# SOURCES.md — where the data comes from, and what that obliges

Every dataset the app reads, with its licence, whether it can be redistributed,
and whether it needs a credential.

`apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/data/README.md` covers the **vendored** datasets
in depth — what each script emits and why. This file is the other half: the
**networked** sources, and the one question that document does not answer, which
is *what happens when the app is given to someone else*.

**Verified** below means someone read the licence page and recorded what it
says. Where it says *not verified*, nobody has — treat it as unknown rather
than permissive, and check before relying on it.

---

## The two standing rules

1. **Never bundle bulk third-party data into the repo or the installer.** Every
   install fetches its own copy. This is already true mechanically — `.gitignore`
   excludes `**/data/*` and the vendored files are regenerated by
   `scripts/vendor-*.mjs` — but it is a rule, not an accident, and it is what
   keeps the non-commercial and no-bulk-distribution terms below satisfiable.
2. **No source may require the user to create an account.** A layer that
   silently does nothing until someone registers with a third party is a broken
   layer for everyone who doesn't. See the SuperMAG entry.

| Credential | **Free personal API key required.** The original design leaned on NASA's shared `DEMO_KEY` as an optional fallback, the same shape as the Kp/Dst credential note above. ### SuperMAG — evaluated and rejected, 2026-08-16

Roughly 180 stations, globally distributed, uniformly processed, with
baseline-subtracted values available directly. Technically the best archive
source of the three. **Not used**, for two reasons:

1. **"Data, plots or derived data products are provided under the limitations of
   'fair use' and cannot be redistributed."** No sample database could ever ship
   with the app, and nothing derived could be published without care.
2. **It requires a per-user account.** Every recipient of this app would have to
   register with JHU/APL and supply a username, or the feature does nothing —
   which violates standing rule 2 above.

Also observed while probing: the service **rate-limits hard and does not recover
quickly** — rapid requests produced server-side `shell_exec` failures that
persisted well past a 90-second backoff. Any client would need to be slow,
resumable and retry-tolerant. That is a fair constraint for a grant-funded
research service, and worth respecting rather than working around.

Its rules also carry an obligation worth remembering if it is ever reconsidered:
where a few stations are **central to a scientific conclusion**, co-authorship
must be offered to those stations' PIs. That would attach directly to H4b, which
is a per-station hypothesis by construction.

Rules of the road: <https://supermag.jhuapl.edu/info/?page=rulesoftheroad>

## Where attribution actually appears

Required credits are surfaced in the app's legend rather than buried here — a
licence condition is not satisfied by a file in the repository that the reader
of a rendered map never sees. `SOURCES.md` is the record of *what is owed*; the
legend is where it is *paid*.
